How it works

A Bézier curve has a start point, two control points and an end point. The control points determine the shape of the curve. The Canvas version draws the paths step by step and fills them when complete.

In SVG, M moves the drawing point, C creates a cubic Bézier curve, L draws a straight line and Z closes the path.
